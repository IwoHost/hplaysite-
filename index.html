01

What Hplay is

Most music apps want an account, a subscription and a connection. Hplay wants a folder.

You point it at music that is already on your device and it plays it, with a wheel you spin instead of a list you scroll. Cover art, albums, artists, playlists, search, a sleep timer, a vinyl view that spins while the song runs, and five different screen looks. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. There is no sign-in, because there is nothing to sign in to.

It behaves like a music app should on a phone. It holds the lock screen with the cover art on it, puts controls in the notification shade, takes the volume keys, pauses when you pull your headphones out, and keeps playing with the screen off.

This is an alpha

Hplay is still being built and tested. Things move, break and get renamed between versions. Keep your own copy of your music, do not treat the player as the only place your playlists live, and tell me when something goes wrong. That is what the alpha is for.

02

How to get it

Hplay is in a closed alpha, so it is handed out one person at a time rather than downloaded from here.

Send me an email and I will send the app straight back to you. It is not on the Play Store yet, so you install it yourself. There is no waiting list and no form to fill in. All I ask is that you tell me afterwards what worked and what did not, which is what the feedback page is for.

Once you have it

Step one

Install it

Your phone will ask whether you trust an app from outside the store. Say yes, and it installs like any other. Nothing to register, no account to make.

Step two

Add music

Point Hplay at a folder of your own files, or add songs one at a time. It reads the tags and the cover art out of the files themselves.

Step three

Spin the wheel

Drag around the wheel to move, press the middle to choose, press MENU to go back. A short guided tour runs the first time so you are not guessing.

No music handy? Take the twenty demo tracks below, drop them in a folder, and point the player at that.

04

Demo music, free to keep

Twenty tracks, six made-up compilations, 51 minutes. Built to give the player something real to chew on.

Testing a music player with three songs tells you nothing. So these were written and synthesised from scratch for Hplay: proper tags, embedded cover art, varied lengths, loud ones and quiet ones. They are not recordings of any real band, and no real artist is involved. The artist names, the track names and the sleeves are all invented. Yours to download, keep and play anywhere.
